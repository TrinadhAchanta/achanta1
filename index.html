FAQ

What partners ask us first.

How fast can we launch?

Typical launch is 4–6 weeks: pad survey, regulatory approvals, hub placement, and API/POS integration.

Do customers need special hardware?

No. We deploy pre-certified pads that fit small footprints. Customers get unlock codes via SMS or your app.

How do we integrate?

Use our REST/GraphQL APIs or direct POS integrations. Webhooks return live events for ETAs, proof-of-delivery, and returns.

Where do you operate?

We run mixed urban and suburban routes. Tell us your next city—we model airspace, pads, and micro-hubs for you.
